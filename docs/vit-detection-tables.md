# ViT-B/16 detection panel

`token_mask @ before_attention_norm`: whole patch tokens dropped from the
residual stream entering attention in every one of the 12 encoder blocks,
CLS never masked. Probe rate per cell is the one whose clean-validation
shift ratio lands closest to 0.8, which needs no labels. PSU is fractional,
one-sided. Quantiles are the false-positive budget, so TPR@10% is the
threshold set at the 10th percentile of clean-validation PSU.

Published values are PSBD (CVPR 2025) on ResNet-18, Table 1 for 10% and the
appendix table for 5%. Blank where the paper has no counterpart: it reports
no CIFAR-100 and never evaluates badnet_a2a, bpp, lf, sig or tact.
Adaptive-Blend is published at 1% (CIFAR-10, GTSRB) and 2% (Tiny) and reused
in both its tables, so it is not rate-matched to our rows.

## Reading the table

This panel is all-to-one only. `badnet_a2a` is excluded because all-to-all
breaks the prediction-shift premise structurally rather than scoring badly
under it: with no single target class for a shifted prediction to collapse
onto, the PSU ordering reverses and AUROC falls below 0.5 on every dataset.
That is a separate result, written up in `docs/all-to-all-inversion.md`.

`tact` is source-specific: it flips only its source classes, so its ASR and
its detection numbers are measured over that population and not over every
non-target image. Measuring over the wider pool divides the true rate by the
class count and makes a working attack look like a failed one.
