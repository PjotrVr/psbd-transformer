\section{Planned mechanism experiments}
\label{sec:planned}

Each entry below is a specification, what is measured, on which checkpoints, with which tool, what falsifies it and what it costs. An entry marked done points at the table or figure it produced in this draft. An entry marked pending carries the command that would produce it, so that the plan cannot drift from the code.

\subsection{Per-attack activation localisation by layer}

\paragraph{Done in part.} The per-layer TAC pass of \cref{tab:tac-layers} ran on \TacLayersCheckpoints{} checkpoints through \code{scripts/paper/run\_tac\_layers.py}, which calls \code{analysis.cases.load\_latent\_case} and \code{analysis.direction} on \TacLayersSamples{} paired images at every block in single precision, and \cref{tab:activation-patching} consolidated the patching records already on disk over every cached cell. What is pending is the same TAC pass over the whole cached panel, at about a minute per checkpoint on the login node, and its per-sublayer decomposition, which the residual stream's linearity makes exact, $d_h^{(l+1)} = d_h^{(l)} + d_a^{(l)} + d_m^{(l)}$, with $d$ the backdoor direction, $a$ the attention write and $m$ the MLP write at layer $l$. The falsifier is a benign reference whose profile matches a backdoored one, and the command is \code{PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/paper/run\_tac\_layers.py --folders <every cached folder>} followed by the generator.

\subsection{Why the attention input beats the MLP input and the residual positions}

The accounts that compete are absorption, routing and margin. The LayerNorm absorption account says a norm following the injection point undoes additive noise and not masking, which explains the operator sign reversal of \cref{tab:operators-deltas} and is measured on a pair of checkpoints at matched injected size, with a survival ratio below the identity for noise and at the identity for masks where a norm follows, and at the identity for both where none does. It says nothing about why the token mask at the attention input beats the token mask at the MLP input by \AttentionInputMinusMlpInputTokenMask{}, since both are followed by a norm. The routing account says attention is the only sublayer that moves a local trigger between tokens, so perturbing what attention reads disturbs the single step with no substitute, and it predicts that the site effect is carried by the local triggers. The margin account says the site sets the Jacobian from the perturbation to the readout and nothing else, and it predicts the same site ordering for every attack. \Cref{tab:family-split-local-global} is the first cut, and the pending measurement is the site effect of \cref{tab:operators-deltas} split by trigger locality, from cache, with the falsifier that a site effect confined to the local triggers refutes the margin account and a uniform one refutes the routing account. Cost is CPU minutes. The absorption measurement itself should extend from the pair of checkpoints to every benign reference and every attack, at seconds each, through \code{experiments/residual\_stream\_mechanism/layernorm\_absorption.py --checkpoint-folder <folders>}.

\subsection{Whether the method works on ViT for a different reason than on ConvNets}

The published account is neuron bias, tested in \cref{sec:shift}. The account this draft leans on is decision margin, and its direct tests have not been run. The first is the smoothed margin of randomized smoothing \citep{cohen2019smoothing}, the gap between the top pair of noise-averaged class probabilities, which the sweep's per-pass softmax would give if the cache stored more than the tracked class, so it needs a sweep with the full per-pass softmax at a larger pass count on the login-node checkpoints, a few GPU minutes each, and the falsifier is a Spearman correlation with per-sample PSU near $0$. The second is the minimum $\ell_2$ flip perturbation of MDTD \citep{rajabi2023mdtd}, a handful of PGD steps per sample at the probe site, a few GPU minutes per checkpoint, with the same falsifier. The third is the certified radius $R = \sigma\,\Phi^{-1}(P_c - \psu)$ that the smoothing bound gives at the probe site with $\sigma$ the noise scale and $\Phi^{-1}$ the standard normal quantile, which is exact rather than a second-order expansion, and it is a CPU recomputation once the first test's sweep exists. The fourth is the confidence-only decomposition already measured on a few cells, that the share of PSU explained by the unperturbed softmax is near $0$ at the input-side probes and near $1$ at the final norm, which turns into a defender-legal probe selection rule and should be read on the panel from cache.

\subsection{The early-band input-side placement}

\Cref{tab:depth-bands} has an empty row, the recommended token mask restricted to blocks \BandEarly{}. The routing account predicts it to be the worst band of its family, since it perturbs before any routing has happened, and the strength account predicts it to be indistinguishable from the other bands at matched shift. Adding the placement is a single entry in \code{configs/psbd\_basis.json}, then \code{PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/coverage\_ledger.py} and \code{PYTHONPATH=. python pbs/generate\_basis\_jobs.py --asr-class clears} followed by the submit script, several GPU hours over the panel, after which \code{scripts/paper/tab\_depth\_bands.py} fills the row without any change. A perturbation propagation panel would read the retained clean-backdoor separation along the reference direction at every layer downstream of each placement at a pair of rates, through \code{models.positions.plug\_dropout} composed with \code{analysis.features.captured\_layers} in a single forward, a few GPU minutes per checkpoint, and the falsifier is the direction being lost before the middle band under a placement that detects well.

\subsection{The operator-matched dropout sweep}

The family split's original operator was dropout, the basis panel carries none at any input-side position, and \cref{tab:family-split} prints its dropout row pending. The sweep is the input-side positions with dropout on the cached cells, about the same cost as the early band, through the same commands with the placements added to the declaration, and the falsifier for the family claim is what the token-mask row already showed.
