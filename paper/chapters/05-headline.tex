\section{Headline results}

\begin{claim}{CRITICAL}{F02}
If PSBD transfers to ViT and the placement matters, a placement chosen by search should beat the published placement on a panel it was not chosen on, at the paper's own rate rule, paired within cell, with an interval excluding $0$ that survives dropping any single attack. A gain confined to a single dataset, rate or attack would mean the search fit the panel.
\end{claim}

\subsection{Transfer}

PSBD works on ViT and gives chance on benign models\finding{STRONG}{F01}. The published placement, dropout after every residual add, reaches a mean AUROC of \PublishedAurocAdaptive{} at the paper's adaptive rule over the \PanelCellsCached{} cached cells, with \PublishedInversions{} cells below chance and a worst cell of \PublishedFloorAuroc{}. The benign references read \ConfidenceNullBenignPsu{} on PSU and \DepthBenignAuroc{} on the prediction-depth statistic of \cref{sec:latent}, so the method reacts to a learned backdoor and not to the presence of a trigger-shaped patch. The transfer is real and it is far from the method's ceiling on this architecture.

\subsection{The recommended placement}

Masking whole tokens at the input of each attention LayerNorm, \code{\RecommendedPlacement}, is the recommended placement\finding{CRITICAL}{F02}. \Cref{tab:headline} gives AUROC and TPR at every budget for it at the adaptive rule and at the matched rule, beside the published placement, and \cref{tab:headline-deltas} gives the paired deltas. Over the panel it reaches \HeadlineAurocAdaptive{} at the adaptive rule and \HeadlineAurocMatched{} at the matched rule, a paired gain over the published placement of \HeadlineGainAdaptiveAuroc{} with a bootstrap interval of [\HeadlineGainAdaptiveAurocLow{}, \HeadlineGainAdaptiveAurocHigh{}], and \HeadlineGainMatchedAuroc{} at matched strength. The gain is \HardGainAdaptiveAuroc{} on the hard attacks and \PrimaryGainAdaptiveAuroc{} on the primary datasets, positive on every dataset and every rate, and largest where the published placement fails, \CifarOneZeroZeroOnePercentGain{} on CIFAR-100 at \PanelRateLow{}. Its TPR at the \LowestQuantilePercent{} budget is \HeadlineTprAtOnePercent{}. \Cref{tab:headline-loo} refits the gain with each attack dropped in turn. The smallest refit is \HeadlineGainMinLeaveOneAttackOutMatched{} at the matched rule with a lower bound of \HeadlineGainMinLeaveOneAttackOutMatchedLow{}, and \HeadlineGainMinLeaveOneAttackOutAdaptive{} at the adaptive rule with a lower bound of \HeadlineGainMinLeaveOneAttackOutAdaptiveLow{}, so the matched-rule claim meets every criterion of the CRITICAL grade and the adaptive-rule claim meets all but the last, its weakest refit's interval touching $0$.

The placement is not without failures, and they are in the table rather than in a footnote. Its worst cell reads \HeadlineFloorAuroc{} and \HeadlineInversions{} cells sit below chance at the adaptive rule, both on CIFAR-10 at \PanelRateHigh{}, SIG and WaNet, and the SIG cell reads well above chance at the matched rule, so a part of the failure is the adaptive rule overshooting the placement's useful range rather than the placement itself. On the full basis ranking of \cref{tab:basis-ranking} the recommended placement is ranked \BasisRankRecommendedAdaptive{} by adaptive-rule mean, behind \code{\BasisBestPlacement} at \BasisBestAurocAdaptive{}, which has no inversion and a higher floor, and it is ranked \BasisRankRecommendedMatched{} at the matched rule. The pair are tied within noise at the deployable rule, and the runner-up is a residual-adjacent position, which matters for \cref{sec:placement}.

\subsection{Seed replicates}

Every panel cell is a single training seed\finding{SUPPORTING}{F03}. \Cref{tab:seeds} reads the \SeedCells{} cached cells on CIFAR-100 and Tiny that were retrained to \SeedsPerCell{} seeds and swept at both placements. The recommended placement's AUROC has a mean seed standard deviation of \SeedSdRecommended{}, the published placement's \SeedSdPublished{}, and the paired gain's \SeedSdGain{} with a worst cell of \SeedSdGainMax{}. Averaged over seeds the gain is \SeedGainMean{} on those cells, and its sign flips across seeds on \SeedGainSignFlips{} of them, every such cell a Blend or WaNet cell where both placements sit near their ceiling and the gain is near $0$. The published placement is the unstable arm. Its spread comes from cells where the adaptive rule lands past its useful range on a seed and inside it on another, and that instability is itself part of the case for a placement whose operating range the rule can aim at.
