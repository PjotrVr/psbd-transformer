\section{What is missing}
\label{sec:missing}

For each headline claim, the evidence it still lacks, followed by the ledger of every macro in this draft with the grade and status of the finding that cites it.

\paragraph{The recommended placement beats the published one.} Every panel cell is a single training seed, and the \SeedCells{} replicated cells of \cref{tab:seeds} cover BadNets, Blend and WaNet on the primary datasets only. The seed replication plan trains further seeds per cell in priority order, primary datasets first, and the decision rule already recorded is that a per-cell seed standard deviation well under the smallest effect claimed carries every remaining claim at \SeedsPerCell{} seeds. The adaptive-rule leave-one-attack-out refit touches $0$ when TaCT is dropped, and it does not at the matched rule. The \PanelCellsAwaitingSweep{} new clearing cells on SVHN and EuroSAT have no sweep yet.

\paragraph{The founding claim and the family split are refuted.} Both refutations rest on the full panel. What is missing is the operator-matched dropout sweep at the input-side positions, so that the family split's original operator is read on the same panel that refuted its token-mask form.

\paragraph{The site effect and the operator sign reversal.} The LayerNorm absorption measurement behind the operator reversal sits on a pair of checkpoints and should extend to every benign reference and attack. The site effect has not been split by trigger locality, which is the measurement that separates the routing account from the margin account.

\paragraph{The depth bands.} The early band of the input-side family has never been swept, and it is the single row that decides between the routing and the strength account of the banding asymmetry.

\paragraph{The prediction shift phenomenon.} The target class is \PanelTargetLabel{} on every panel cell and the benign references fall back onto that class, so the measurement is controlled against the benign share rather than against a different target. The GTSRB clean-label cells at target class \SigGtsrbTargetClass{} exist and carry no detection cache, so the table that reads the same share at a second target class is empty.

\paragraph{The latent chain.} The causal ablation is \AblationCheckpoints{} checkpoints on CIFAR-10, the crystallisation reading is a pair of attacks on the primary datasets, the patching null is uninformative for global triggers, and the direct margin tests have not been run. The per-layer TAC pass covers \TacLayersCheckpoints{} checkpoints and should cover the cached panel.

\paragraph{The competitor detectors.} Smoke-tested on \SmokeCheckpoints{} checkpoints. The panel sweep is generated and unsubmitted, and it is the largest pending compute item.

\paragraph{Swin.} No coverage ledger, no basis sweep, unbalanced coverage, single seed, no Tiny cell, and \SwinSeedReplicates{} seed replicates with no detection cache.

\paragraph{The clean-label attacks.} SVHN and EuroSAT implant at the middle and high rates and carry no detection cache. Label-Consistent enters on CIFAR-10 alone. The \GtsrbDivergedRuns{} diverged GTSRB runs need reruns under a recipe with clipping or a schedule, and the dirty-label controls at the larger target class among them are what would settle whether the target class switch is a confound.
