\section{Experimental setup}

\subsection{The panel}

The ViT panel declares \PanelCellsTotal{} cells, a cell per dataset, attack and poison rate over CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, GTSRB, Tiny ImageNet and, since the clean-label work of \cref{sec:cleanlabel}, SVHN and EuroSAT, \PanelAttacksDeclared{} attacks and \PanelRates{} poisoning, all at target class \PanelTargetLabel{}. The attacks are BadNets \citep{gu2017badnets}, Blend \citep{chen2017targeted}, low-frequency and BPP triggers, WaNet \citep{nguyen2021wanet}, TaCT \citep{tang2021demon}, and the clean-label attacks SIG and Label-Consistent \citep{turner2019label}, plus Adaptive-Blend. A cell enters a detection table only when its attack success rate reaches \AsrBar{}, and \PanelCellsClearing{} of the \PanelCellsTotal{} do, over \PanelAttacksClearing{} attacks, while \PanelCellsDiverged{} cells whose training diverged are excluded from every table. Of the clearing cells, \PanelCellsCached{} on \PanelDatasetsCached{} datasets carry every placement of the basis today and \PanelCellsAwaitingSweep{} on the new datasets await their sweep, so every detection number in this paper spans the \PanelCellsCached{} cached cells. \Cref{tab:panel} prints every declared cell with its attack success and clean accuracy, and marks the cells below the bar rather than dropping them, so a reader can see that WaNet never implants at \PanelRateLow{}, that Adaptive-Blend never clears the bar on ViT at any rate, and that SIG clears it on CIFAR-10 at \PanelRateHigh{} alone. Every cached cell carries all \BasisSize{} placements of the basis, since a ranking computed over whichever cells happened to carry a placement ranks the cells and not the placements, which is a mistake this project made and corrected before any number here was read.

\subsection{Training and naming}

Every checkpoint is fine-tuned from ImageNet weights for \TrainingEpochs{} epochs with Adam, the same budget on every dataset for comparability, at a single training seed except where a chapter says otherwise. Folder names follow a single template, architecture, dataset, attack, rate and optional tags, and every folder carries a sidecar with the dataset, attack, label mode, target, requested and realised poison rate, seed, commit and timestamps, from which the eval set is rebuilt in memory. Attack success at eval time counts the triggered images whose source class is not already the target, which for a clean-label attack is the complement of the training eligibility rule, and a benign reference per dataset is probed with the \BenignProbeAttack{} trigger at target \BenignProbeTarget{} so that every detection number has a chance-level control on the same images.

\subsection{Coverage and provenance}

The coverage ledger diffs the declaration against the results tree and is the single source of which cells and placements exist, so a table cannot be built on a placement that was never cached. Every table and figure in this paper names its generator, its inputs, the commit and the time in its first line, every number in the prose is a macro with a provenance record, and a chapter that carries a typed digit fails the build. The sweep has a GPU stage writing the per-pass probabilities and a CPU stage reading them, so every scoring rule in this paper was recomputed from the same tensors and a recomputation that disagreed with a recorded number was treated as an audit finding rather than as noise.
