\section{The clean-label difficulties}
\label{sec:cleanlabel}

\begin{claim}{NEGATIVE}{F22}
A clean-label attack keeps every label, so it can only poison images that already carry the target label, and its poison rate is capped at the target class's share of the training set. The panel's rates were declared without that cap, and most of the clean-label cells this project trained were replicates of a single configuration mislabelled as a rate curve. This chapter records the cap, the fixes tried and what each bought, because a detection number on an attack that did not implant is not a detection number.
\end{claim}

\subsection{The cap}

On CIFAR-100 the cap is \PanelRateLow{}, on Tiny half of it, and on GTSRB at the panel's target class it is a fraction of a percent, so \PanelRateHigh{} clean-label poisoning is reachable on CIFAR-10 alone and every clean-label GTSRB, CIFAR-100 and Tiny cell above the cap trained the identical poisoned index set. The realised rate was recovered into every sidecar by replaying the seeded selection, and the collapse produced free replicates whose spread, above a third of an ASR point on identical configurations, is the honest single-run error bar of this project. GTSRB is the only dataset whose class sizes are uneven, so moving its clean-label target to class \SigGtsrbTargetClass{}, the largest, lifts the cap tenfold and buys back the \PanelRateLow{} and \PanelRateMid{} cells, and those runs carry a target tag in their folder names.

\subsection{SIG on GTSRB at the larger target class}

\Cref{tab:clean-label-sig-gtsrb} reads the \SigGtsrbRuns{} runs, \SigGtsrbRates{} rates at \SigGtsrbSeeds{} seeds. The rate is realised at every rate, and the attack still does not clear the bar on the mean at any of them. At \PanelRateMid{} the mean over \SigGtsrbTlOneFivePercentSeeds{} usable seeds is \SigGtsrbTlOneFivePercentMeanAsr{} and \SigGtsrbTlOneFivePercentSeedsClearing{} seed clears both bars, the seed whose loss spiked in its last epoch. The cap was part of the failure and it was not the whole failure, and SIG on GTSRB stays out of the panel.

\subsection{Diverged training runs}

\GtsrbDivergedRuns{} of the \GtsrbRunsScored{} ViT GTSRB training runs with a recorded clean accuracy ended below half the benign reference, every such run on GTSRB and none on any other dataset or on Swin. Each sat at the loss floor for consecutive epochs and then, within a single epoch, jumped to chance-level cross-entropy and stayed there, ending as a constant predictor. The recipe has a fixed Adam step, no schedule, no warmup and no clipping, GTSRB is the only dataset whose inputs are not standardised, and it is the dataset on which the loss reaches the floor within the budget on most runs, which is the setting in which a step after many near-zero gradients divides a fresh gradient by a decayed second moment. The trainer prints validation accuracy and never reads it back, every job ends with an unconditional zero exit, so the wrecked weights were saved and reported as success. The diverged runs are excluded from every mean in this chapter and the reruns are listed in \cref{sec:missing}.

\subsection{Label-Consistent with its adversarial bases}

Turner et al. perturb each base image adversarially before stamping the patch, so the image stops supporting its own label and the trigger becomes the only reliable cue, and the PSBD paper used precomputed adversarial images throughout. This project's Label-Consistent lacked that step, which is why its patch-only variant needed the whole target class before it implanted. The bases are now generated by untargeted PGD against each dataset's benign surrogate at \LcEpsilonBudgets{} budgets, and \cref{tab:clean-label-lc-epsilon} is the pilot, the first seed at each dataset's cap rate. The rule picks the smallest budget clearing the attack success bar inside the clean-accuracy drop bar, and it picks a budget on \LcDatasetsWithEpsilon{} dataset, \LcEpsilonChosen{}. On CIFAR-10 every budget clears the attack and breaks the clean-accuracy bar by a class's worth, since \PanelRateHigh{} is the whole target class, on CIFAR-100 and Tiny no budget implants at the cap, and on GTSRB \LcGtsrbPilotDiverged{} of the \LcGtsrbPilotRuns{} pilot runs diverged. Label-Consistent enters the panel on CIFAR-10 alone, at a clean-accuracy cost the bar rejects, and its earlier patch-only cells stay on disk without competing for the slot.

\subsection{Multi-target SIG and the new datasets}

Widening the clean-label target set to the first \MultitargetSetSizes{} classes multiplies the cap by the set size. \Cref{tab:clean-label-multitarget} reads the probe on the primary datasets at \PanelRateLow{}, \MultitargetSeeds{} seeds per set, where the recorded attack success counts a prediction landing anywhere in the set. \MultitargetRunsClearing{} of the \MultitargetRuns{} runs clear the bar. The widening buys attack success on Tiny and on CIFAR-100 and not enough of it, and the set-wide success is not the panel's criterion, so none of these cells enters the panel. SVHN and EuroSAT were trained because their target classes are large enough for every panel rate. \Cref{tab:clean-label-new-datasets} reads SIG at every rate with a Blend control, \NewDatasetSeeds{} seeds per cell, and \NewDatasetCellsClearingOnMean{} of the \NewDatasetCells{} cells clear on the seed mean, every SIG cell at \PanelRateMid{} and above and no SIG cell at \PanelRateLow{}. These are the first clean-label cells outside CIFAR-10 that implant at a panel rate, they are admitted to the coverage panel as the \PanelCellsAwaitingSweep{} clearing cells that await their sweep, and they carry no detection cache yet.
