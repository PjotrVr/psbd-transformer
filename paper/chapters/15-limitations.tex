\subsection{What the method does not cover}

PSBD needs a single target class for the triggered prediction to sit far from every boundary, and an all-to-all label map removes it\finding{STRONG}{F20}. \Cref{tab:all-to-all} reads fractional PSU at the recommended placement beside the statistics of the unperturbed softmax on the \ATwoaCells{} all-to-all cells, the all-to-one cells of the same record and the benign references. PSU reads \ATwoaPsuAuroc{} on all-to-all against \ATwooPsuAuroc{} on all-to-one, and the negative entropy of the unperturbed softmax, which the sweep already stores and discards, reads \ATwoaEntropyAuroc{} there against \ATwooEntropyAuroc{} on all-to-one, with the benign references at \BenignEntropyAuroc{}. The statistics fail at opposite ends, and a label-free router that picks between them by the sign of the suspect pool's mean PSU against clean validation gains \RouterDelta{} with interval \RouterDeltaCi{} over always choosing PSU on \RouterCells{} cells, against an oracle of \RouterOracle{}. The all-to-all inversion is reported as a diagnostic and never as detection, since choosing which tail to flag from the AUROC needs the labels the detector exists to predict, and the same content dependence sweeps continuously with the number of target classes, a framing anticipated by \citet{wang2025a2x}.

\subsection{The adaptive attacker}

An attacker who trains against the deployed probe collapses it\finding{STRONG}{F21}. \Cref{tab:adaptive-attacker} reads the \AdaptiveVitCells{} ViT cells whose evasive checkpoint keeps attack success at or above \AdaptiveHighAsr{}. The probed operator falls from \AdaptiveVitProbedBase{} to \AdaptiveVitProbedEvade{} at a clean-accuracy cost of \AdaptiveVitCaCost{}, the operators the attacker never trained against still read \AdaptiveVitTransfer{} on the same checkpoints, and the best union of probes reads \AdaptiveVitUnion{}. Operator diversity is defence in depth, and its limits are recorded rather than assumed. A probed operator does not merely stop working but inverts, a minimum-rank union adopts the inversion, a median-rank union that fixed that on synthetic probes loses on the real evasive checkpoints, neither union beats the best single probe on AUROC, and the attacker in this record hinges on the clean mean PSU where the defender thresholds on a quantile, so a competent attacker is stronger than the one measured.

\subsection{Withdrawn and refuted claims}

Several conclusions of this project were overturned by a strength-matched comparison, and a headline was withdrawn\finding{NEGATIVE}{F23}. The founding pre-residual claim of \cref{sec:placement}, an early reading that the published placement fails by saturating, and the earlier reading of the family split all compared a placement inside its operating range against another outside it. An earlier headline of a large gain for the IBD-PSC amplification at the MLP norm output read the winner at a clean shift ratio near saturation and the baseline far below it, a strength gap the size of the reported effect, and on the basis panel that placement ranks below the recommended one at both rules in \cref{tab:basis-ranking} with the lowest floor of any operator that reaches every cell. A batch-coupled gaussian operator inflated every gaussian record before it was corrected and re-swept, which moved the gaussian control from the top of the operator ranking to the middle without changing the conclusion that removal is not required. Every attempt to exploit where the backdoor sits, random and targeted head masks, a \VitTotalHeads{}-head sensitivity profile and coordinate ablation, failed, and the causal ablation of \cref{sec:latent} is the reason. The onset-based band rule failed a pre-registered held-out test. WaNet never implants at \PanelRateLow{} and Adaptive-Blend never clears the bar on ViT, so the panel's hardest attacks are absent from its detection tables by construction, and every item here is in \cref{tab:hypothesis-ledger}.

\subsection{Scope}

Every number is ViT-B/16 or Swin-S fine-tuned from ImageNet for \TrainingEpochs{} epochs with a plain Adam recipe, at a single seed except where stated, on \PanelDatasetsDeclared{} datasets whose largest has \ClassesTiny{} classes, against data-poisoning attacks at \PanelRates{} poisoning, scored on a held-out test pool rather than on the poisoned training set the paper scores. The threat model grants a clean validation split of \HeldoutSize{} images and at most \ForwardPasses{} extra forward passes per input, and a defender paying \CdLPasses{} passes buys a different comparison. The margin account is stated as what the method needs and does not need, and its direct tests are the first items of \cref{sec:planned}.
