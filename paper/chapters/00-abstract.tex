\begin{abstract}
Prediction Shift Backdoor Detection (PSBD) flags a poisoned input by how little its prediction moves when dropout is switched on at inference, and its authors explain the effect by a neuron bias that pulls clean inputs toward the attacker's target class. Both the method and the explanation were established on ConvNets. We port PSBD to ViT-B/16 and Swin-S and treat the port as a search over where the perturbation is injected and what is injected. On a panel of \PanelCellsClearing{} implanted cells over \PanelDatasetsDeclared{} datasets, \PanelAttacksDeclared{} attacks and \PanelRates{} poisoning, of which \PanelCellsCached{} cells on \PanelDatasetsCached{} datasets carry every placement today, the published placement, dropout after every residual add, reaches a mean AUROC of \PublishedAurocAdaptive{} at the paper's own rate rule. Masking whole tokens at the input of each attention block reaches \HeadlineAurocAdaptive{}, a paired gain of \HeadlineGainAdaptiveAuroc{} with a bootstrap interval of [\HeadlineGainAdaptiveAurocLow{}, \HeadlineGainAdaptiveAurocHigh{}], and its seed spread is \SeedSdRecommended{} against \SeedSdPublished{} for the published placement. The project's founding claim, that dropout before the residual add beats dropout after it, reads \PreMinusPostMatched{} on the same panel and is refuted. The family split that replaced it, sub-layer inputs against residual-adjacent positions, reads \FamilyGapMatched{} once the operator is held fixed and does not survive either. What carries the gain is a single specific placement, and its nearest explanations are measured: a LayerNorm following the position absorbs additive noise and not masking, and attention is the only sublayer that can move a local trigger from its own patches to the class token, which activation patching locates entirely in those patches through the first half of the network. The prediction shift toward the target class exists on ViT for local triggers on few-class datasets and is absent for global triggers, while detection works in both regimes, so the neuron bias account does not carry the method here. We report every negative result, the single-seed status of most cells and the pending competitor panel.
\end{abstract}
