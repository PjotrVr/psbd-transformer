\section{Competitor detectors}
\label{sec:competitors}

\begin{claim}{WEAK}{F18}
A placement result is only worth reporting against the input-level detectors a defender would otherwise deploy, on the same splits, the same threshold rule and the same quantile grid. \RegistryDetectors{} detectors are ported and registered, each with a numerical cross-check against its reference implementation, and they have been run on \SmokeCheckpoints{} checkpoints as an acceptance test. The panel sweep is pending, and nothing in this chapter is a comparison.
\end{claim}

The registry holds STRIP \citep{gao2019strip}, SCALE-UP in both of its variants \citep{guo2023scale}, IBD-PSC in its faithful form and a calibrated form \citep{hou2024ibdpsc}, TeCo \citep{liu2023teco}, CD-L \citep{huang2023distilling}, Beatrix \citep{ma2023beatrix}, TED \citep{mo2024ted}, SentiNet \citep{chou2020sentinet} and the max-softmax confidence as the null model. Every detector returns a score where low means poisoned, is negated at most once at its own boundary, reads the same \HeldoutSize{}-image clean split PSBD reads, and states its forward passes per input, from a single pass for the confidence null, Beatrix and TED to \CdLPasses{} for CD-L and \SentinetPasses{} for SentiNet, which is a deployment cost a comparison table has to carry beside its AUROC.

\Cref{tab:detector-smoke} is the smoke run, \SmokeDetectors{} detectors on the GTSRB benign reference and on the \SmokeBackdooredCheckpoints{} backdoored cells at \PanelRateMid{}, BadNets and Blend. The acceptance criterion was chance on the benign reference, which every detector meets between \SmokeBenignAurocMin{} and \SmokeBenignAurocMax{}, and separation in the expected direction on the backdoored cells. STRIP reads \SmokeStripBadnetAuroc{} on BadNets and TED \SmokeTedBlendAuroc{} on Blend, the confidence null alone reaches \SmokeConfidenceBlendAuroc{} on Blend, and a pair of detectors failed acceptance. IBD-PSC reads \SmokeIbdPscBadnetAuroc{} on BadNets and \SmokeIbdPscBadnetFullAuroc{} on the full split, because the paper's amplification factor collapses a ConvNet's clean accuracy within a few layers while on a ViT it scales a single branch input of a residual block and a model at high accuracy keeps every prediction through every amplified layer, so the faithful port saturates and a calibrated variant that searches the factor upward runs beside it. SentiNet reads \SmokeSentinetBadnetAuroc{} on BadNets, inverted, because its class-activation mask covers none of the trigger's pixels at any block of the ViT. Beatrix reads \SmokeBeatrixBadnetAuroc{} at the truncated split and \SmokeBeatrixBadnetFullAuroc{} at the full one, so its chance reading was the truncation and not the port, which is the reason the detectors that fit per-class statistics are rerun at the full split before being judged. \Cref{tab:detector-smoke-followups} records the \SmokeFollowups{} follow-ups.
