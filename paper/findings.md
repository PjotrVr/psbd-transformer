# Findings registry

Every finding once. A claim names its numbers by macro (`\HeadlineGainAdaptiveAuroc`), never by digit, so
the registry cannot drift from the tables. Grades follow the scale in README.md. `status` is
one of cached (computed from data on disk), login-node (needs a measurement on a handful of
checkpoints), pbs (needs a cluster sweep) or pending. `draft` is one of main, appendix,
limitations or omitted, and says what the draft foregrounds, nothing is deleted. The chapter
text under `chapters/` is the primary record and this table is the machine index of it.
