\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Input-level detectors.}
The defenses closest to ours decide per input at test time from the deployed model and a small clean set. STRIP \cite{gao2019strip} superimposes clean images and reads prediction entropy, SCALE-UP \cite{guo2023scale} amplifies pixels and IBD-PSC \cite{hou2024ibdpsc} normalization parameters and read what survives, TeCo \cite{liu2023teco} reads the corruption severity at which a prediction flips, CD-L \cite{huang2023distilling} distills a minimal input mask, Beatrix \cite{ma2023beatrix} reads Gram-matrix deviations, TED \cite{mo2024ted} reads the rank trajectory of an input's neighbors across layers and SentiNet \cite{chou2020sentinet} transplants a salient region. PSBD \cite{li2025psbd} belongs to the consistency family with STRIP, SCALE-UP and TeCo, which perturb the model or its input and read how stable the prediction is. To our knowledge no earlier work treats the perturbation site of such a detector as a design choice, as it must be on a ViT. Doan et al.~\cite{doan2023vitpatch} defend ViTs by transforming patches before the positional encoding, the closest ViT-specific input defense.

\paragraph{Training-time defenses.}
Detectors that need the poisoned training pool \cite{tran2018spectral,chen2018activation,tang2021demon,hayase2021spectre} and defenses that retrain or prune the model \cite{liu2018finepruning,li2021nad,wu2021anp,zeng2022ibau,zheng2022clp} answer a different question and need access we do not assume.

\paragraph{Mechanistic work on ViT backdoors.}
Karayal\c{c}{\i}n et al.~\cite{karayalcin2026backdoor} find a trigger direction in ViT activations and remove the backdoor by orthogonalizing it out of the weights, which we reproduce (\cref{sec:mechanism:picek}). Activation patching \cite{zhang2024patching} and the logit lens \cite{nostalgebraist2020logitlens,belrose2023tunedlens} are the tools we use to locate the backdoor by depth.
