\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Models and training.}
Every model is a torchvision \cite{paszke2019pytorch} ViT-B/16 or Swin-S with ImageNet weights, fine-tuned for \TrainingEpochs{} epochs with Adam \cite{kingma2015adam} at a constant learning rate of $10^{-4}$ and weight decay $10^{-4}$, batch size \TrainingBatchSize{}, no dropout and no augmentation beyond the dataset's normalization. Every input is resized to \ModelInputSize{} by \ModelInputSize{} pixels before the backbone.

\paragraph{Datasets and attacks.}
The datasets are CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100 \cite{krizhevsky2009cifar}, GTSRB \cite{stallkamp2012gtsrb} and Tiny ImageNet \cite{le2015tiny}, with SVHN \cite{netzer2011svhn} and EuroSAT \cite{helber2019eurosat} added for the clean-label attacks that the other datasets cannot carry. The attacks are BadNets \cite{gu2017badnets}, Blend \cite{chen2017targeted}, WaNet \cite{nguyen2021wanet}, BPP \cite{wang2022bpp}, the low-frequency attack LF \cite{zeng2021lf}, TaCT \cite{tang2021demon}, SIG \cite{barni2019sig}, Label-Consistent \cite{turner2019label} and Adaptive-Blend \cite{qi2023adaptive}, each at poison rates of 1\%, 5\% and 10\% of the training set. \Cref{app:attacks} describes every attack and the exact trigger and parameters we used.

\paragraph{Which models are evaluated.}
A backdoored model enters the detection results only if its ASR is at least \AsrBar{} and its clean accuracy sits no further than \CleanDropBar{} below the benign reference on the same dataset. A detector cannot be credited or blamed for a backdoor that was never planted. Of the \PanelCellsTotal{} models we trained, \PanelCellsClearing{} pass this bar and \PanelCellsCached{} of those carry enough of the basis to be compared across placements. The results of \cref{sec:results} are read on those \PanelCellsCached{} models, and \cref{app:results} lists every one with its ASR, clean accuracy and detection numbers.

\paragraph{Splits and uncertainty.}
A fixed permutation of each test set gives a clean validation set of \HeldoutSize{} images for the threshold and the rate rule, and an analysis pool for the paired clean and triggered sets. Nothing from the validation set enters the analysis pool. Every gain between 2 placements is a paired difference on the same model, averaged over models, with a 95\% bootstrap interval \cite{efron1993bootstrap} over models from \BootstrapResamples{} resamples. To guard against selecting the best placement on the same models it is reported on, the basis declares a split before any sweep, and \cref{app:protocol} shows that the winning placement holds on the held-out half.
