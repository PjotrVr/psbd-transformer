\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

PSBD transfers to Vision Transformers, and the transfer is decided by placement. Masking whole tokens at the input of every attention block detects backdoored inputs with a mean AUROC of 0.935 over 65 ViT-B/16 cells and 0.968 over 53 Swin-S cells, against 0.832 and 0.871 for the placement the original paper used, with the largest gains at 1\% poisoning. The reason is a property of how a transformer carries a backdoor: as 1 direction, written in the last third of the network and routed through attention from the trigger's tokens to the class token, so a perturbation that removes tokens before attention removes the shortcut while a perturbation on the residual stream perturbs the decision itself. The same reading explains why additive noise loses at the attention input and wins at the MLP input, and why the original paper's prediction shift phenomenon appears only for local triggers on few-class datasets. A single probe is defeated by an attacker who trains against it, and a union over probes is the defence. What remains open is the attacker who trains against the union, the full comparison to other detectors on the hard attacks, and the all-to-all case.
