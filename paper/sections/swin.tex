\section{Transfer to Swin-S}
\label{sec:swin}

Swin-S \cite{liu2021swin} restricts attention to shifted windows of 7 by 7 tokens and merges patches between its 4 stages, so tokens reach every other token only through merging. That tests the account of \cref{sec:mechanism}. If a token mask works because it removes evidence before attention routes it, windowing should not matter, since every route still passes through a windowed attention. We read the same placements on \SwinCells{} backdoored Swin-S models over \SwinDatasets{} datasets, and \SwinPlacementsReported{} placements carry at least 5 of them (\cref{tab:swin-placements}).

\paragraph{The ranking transfers.}
Token masking at the attention input reads a mean AUROC of \SwinRecommendedAurocAdaptive{} over \SwinRecommendedN{} models against \SwinPublishedAurocAdaptive{} for dropout after both residual adds, a paired gain of \SwinGainRecommendedMinusPublished{} [\SwinGainRecommendedMinusPublishedLow{}, \SwinGainRecommendedMinusPublishedHigh{}], close to the \HeadlineGainAdaptiveAuroc{} on ViT. It flags \SwinRecommendedTprOneZero{} of triggered inputs at a 10\% false-positive budget against \SwinPublishedTprOneZero{}. Moving dropout to the attention input with the operator held fixed gains \SwinGainDropoutInputMinusPublished{} [\SwinGainDropoutInputMinusPublishedLow{}, \SwinGainDropoutInputMinusPublishedHigh{}], an interval containing 0, while changing the operator at that site to a token mask gains \SwinGainRecommendedMinusSameSiteDropout{}. On Swin too the attention input rewards removing whole tokens, not the site alone.

\paragraph{The choice is licensed on Swin's selection half.}
On ViT the recommended placement is tied with its twin on the attention branch output, and the declared protocol's selection half leans to the twin by \GainsSelectionHalfTwinGap{} (\cref{app:protocol}). On Swin the twin reads \SwinSelectionHalfTwinAuroc{} against \SwinSelectionHalfRecommendedAuroc{} on the selection half, a gap of \SwinSelectionHalfTwinGap{} that holds at \SwinReportHalfTwinGap{} on the report half, so the protocol run on Swin picks the placement we recommend.
