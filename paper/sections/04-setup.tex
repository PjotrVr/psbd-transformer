\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Models and training.}
Every model is a torchvision ViT-B/16 or Swin-S with ImageNet weights, fine-tuned for 15 epochs with Adam at a constant learning rate of $10^{-4}$ and weight decay $10^{-4}$, batch size 128, no dropout and no augmentation beyond the dataset's normalisation. Training a backdoored model takes 45 to 170 minutes on 1 A100 depending on the dataset. A training run that collapsed in its last epochs, which happened on 13 GTSRB runs, is excluded and rerun with a cosine schedule (\cref{sec:limitations}).

\paragraph{Datasets, attacks and poison rates.}
The datasets are CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, GTSRB and Tiny ImageNet, with SVHN and EuroSAT added for the clean-label attacks that the other datasets cannot carry. The attacks are BadNets \cite{gu2017badnets} (a 3 by 3 patch, all-to-one), Blend \cite{chen2017targeted}, WaNet \cite{nguyen2021wanet}, BPP, low-frequency (LF), TaCT \cite{tang2021demon}, SIG and Label-Consistent \cite{turner2019label}, at poison rates of 1\%, 5\% and 10\%. We call BadNets, Blend and LF the easy attacks and BPP, WaNet, TaCT, SIG, Label-Consistent and Adaptive-Blend the hard ones.

A backdoored model enters the evaluation only if its attack success rate is at least 0.85 and its clean accuracy is within 5 points of the benign reference on the same dataset. This leaves 71 of the 105 models we trained. 65 of them have been swept over every placement of the basis, and every number in \cref{sec:results} is read on those 65. \Cref{tab:panel} in the appendix lists every model.

\paragraph{Splits.}
A fixed permutation of each test set gives a 2000-image clean validation set for the threshold and the rate rule, and an analysis pool for the paired clean and triggered sets. The triggered set contains every analysis image whose class the attack can flip, so a clean-label attack is read on non-target images and an all-to-one attack on every non-target image. Nothing from the validation set enters the analysis pool.

\paragraph{Selection protocol.}
Choosing the best of 18 placements on the same models it is then reported on would be an optimistic reading, so the basis declares a split before any sweep. Placements are selected on CIFAR-10 and GTSRB and reported on CIFAR-100 and Tiny ImageNet. At the matched rule attention-input token masking ranks first on the 33 selection models with a mean AUROC of 0.933, and on the 32 held-out models it reads 0.918 at the matched rule and 0.944 at the adaptive rule. Its lead is therefore not a product of the selection. The numbers in \cref{sec:results} pool both halves, since the held-out reading is if anything higher.

\paragraph{Uncertainty.}
Every gain is a paired difference within a model, and every interval is a 95\% bootstrap interval over models with 5000 resamples. 14 backdoored models have 3 training seeds each, and \cref{sec:robustness:seeds} reports how much a seed moves a placement.
