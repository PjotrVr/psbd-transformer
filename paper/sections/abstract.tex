\begin{abstract}
Prediction Shift Backdoor Detection (PSBD) flags a poisoned input by how little its
prediction moves when the network is perturbed at inference. It was built for ResNets,
where the residual stream is the one obvious place to put that perturbation. A
transformer block offers many places and several kinds of perturbation, and we show that
this choice, rather than the method, decides whether the detector works. Masking whole
tokens at the input of every attention block reaches a mean AUROC of
\HeadlineAurocAdaptive{} over \PanelCellsCached{} backdoored ViT-B/16 models spanning
\PanelDatasetsCached{} datasets and poison rates of \PanelRates{}, against
\PublishedAurocAdaptive{} for the placement the original work used, a paired gain of
\HeadlineGainAdaptiveAuroc{} [\HeadlineGainAdaptiveAurocLow{},
\HeadlineGainAdaptiveAurocHigh{}] that grows to \HardGainAdaptiveAuroc{} on the attacks
that resist detection. It transfers to Swin-S, where windowed attention leaves the
ranking intact over \SwinCells{} models. Against \DetectorsComparedCount{} ported
input-level detectors on the same models and splits, the original placement ranks
\DetectorsAurocRankPublished{} of \DetectorsAurocDefensesRanked{} defenses and ours
ranks \DetectorsAurocRankOurs{}, by \DetectorsAurocMargin{} over the strongest of
them, which is a tie rather than a lead. Site and operator are separate axes, and the operator is decided by
the side of the LayerNorm the perturbation lands on: injected before one, Gaussian noise
costs \StaircaseOperatorsBeforeAttentionNormGaussianGain{} AUROC against token masking
at the same site, and injected after a LayerNorm the 2 tie. We explain that by locating the
backdoor, which is 1 direction in the residual stream, written in the last third of the
network and routed through attention from the trigger's own tokens to the class token.
Removing whole tokens before attention removes that route, while noise at the same site
is renormalized away. We also report the limits. An attacker who trains against 1 probe
defeats it, a union of probes restores detection and all-to-all attacks remain open.
\end{abstract}
