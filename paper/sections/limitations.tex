\section{Scope and limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The results are read on \PanelCellsCached{} ViT-B/16 and \SwinCells{} Swin-S models fine-tuned from ImageNet weights for \TrainingEpochs{} epochs, on datasets of small images.

\paragraph{All-to-all attacks.}
PSBD-TM reads a mean AUROC of \ATwoaPsuAuroc{} over the \ATwoaCells{} all-to-all BadNets models against \ATwooPsuAuroc{} for the all-to-one form. An all-to-all backdoor has no single direction, since each source class pushes toward a different target, so the shortcut is not the firm single decision the statistic relies on. An entropy statistic over the perturbed predictions recovers \ATwoaEntropyAuroc{} and is a route we leave open.

\paragraph{The weakest models.}
PSBD-TM's floor is \HeadlineFloorAuroc{}, on SIG and WaNet on CIFAR-10 at \PanelRateHigh{} poisoning. SIG's shift ratio rises so quickly with the mask rate that the target lands on a rate that disturbs clean predictions as much as poisoned ones. WaNet moves every patch a little rather than writing a few tokens, so no token's removal breaks the shortcut and placements that perturb the residual stream catch it instead. Adding PSBD-RD as a second probe reads that WaNet model at \ProbeUnionWanetCifarOneZeroTmRd{} against \ProbeUnionWanetCifarOneZeroTm{} alone, the clearest case for a union of probes. The weak reading reproduces under 3 mask seeds and on BackdoorBench's own reference model \cite{wu2022backdoorbench}.

\paragraph{Clean-label and adaptive-blend attacks.}
Adaptive-Blend never reaches the attack success bar on ViT, so no detection number includes it. A clean-label attack can poison only its target class, which caps its rate at that class's share of the training set (\cref{app:attacks}), and within those caps SIG implants only on CIFAR-10, SVHN and EuroSAT and Label-Consistent only on GTSRB. The clean-label evidence is therefore thin.

\paragraph{Seeds and the selection protocol.}
Most models have 1 training seed. On the \SeedCells{} models with \SeedsPerCell{} seeds PSBD-TM moves by \SeedSdRecommended{} AUROC between seeds, small against every gain we report (\cref{app:cost}). On ViT the declared selection protocol would pick the branch-output twin, and the recommendation rests on Swin, seed stability and the mechanism (\cref{app:protocol}). \GtsrbDivergedRuns{} of the \GtsrbRunsScored{} GTSRB runs we trained collapsed in their last epochs and are excluded.

\paragraph{Adaptive attackers.}
A single probe can be defeated and the union of probes is not defeated by the same attacker (\cref{sec:robustness:adaptive}). An attacker who trains against the union, and a trigger designed to survive token masking, are untested.
